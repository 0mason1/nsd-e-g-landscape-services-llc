What We Do

Comprehensive Landscaping Services

We handle everything your property needs to look its absolute best — season after season.

🌿

Lawn Maintenance

Consistent mowing, edging, trimming, and blowing to keep your lawn looking sharp every week. We tailor cutting schedules to your grass type and the Charlotte climate.

🌳

Landscape Design & Installation

Transform your outdoor space with custom planting plans, fresh beds, ornamental trees, and seasonal color that enhances curb appeal and property value.

✂️

Tree & Shrub Trimming

Precision pruning and shaping keeps your trees and shrubs healthy, safe, and beautiful. We remove dead growth and shape overgrowth before it becomes a hazard.

🍂

Mulching & Bed Cleanup

Fresh mulch refreshes your beds, retains moisture, and suppresses weeds. We install premium hardwood mulch and handle full seasonal bed cleanups year-round.

💧

Irrigation Services

Properly watered lawns thrive. We install, repair, and optimize sprinkler systems to deliver the right amount of water at the right time — saving you money and effort.

🍃

Seasonal Cleanups

Spring and fall cleanups including leaf removal, debris haul-away, bed preparation, and aeration to give your lawn a healthy reset heading into any season.
